## 2. Engine Optimizations & Research Paper Accelerations

- **Lossless CUDA & host launch reductions (`scala-torch` `0.1.3-gh53`)**:
  - `CudaDriver.scala` & `Cublas.scala`: converted kernel launch and cuBLAS hot paths to `invokeExact` with pre-allocated memory segments/cells.
  - `CudaSession.scala` & `half.cu`: shared scratch arena & `ArgBuilder` (`launchFast` / `hexecuteFast`), zero-alloc `check()`, vectorized `half2` elementwise & GroupNorm stats, fused `h_groupnorm_apply_silu`, NCHW ResNet epilogues (`h_add_nchw_bias*`), batched `1x1` convolutions (`gemmHalfStridedBatched`), shared-memory `h_im2col_3x3_s1_p1_batch`, raised attention scratch cap (`mergedCap`), and FlashAttention-2 multi-warp kernels (`h_fa2_w4r1c*` / `h_fa2_w4r2c*`, `ex2.approx.ftz.f32`) dispatched only where strictly faster than `h_attention_fused_*`.
  - `CudaDiffusionRuntime.scala`: fixed dynamic-context K/V cache leak (`cacheContextKV=true` only on cross-attention `attn2`), added `eq`-based `keptContextsByRef` avoiding per-step `toCpu` DMA syncs, enabled fused NCHW `resnet` & `normOut`+`silu`, and skipped `outputScale == 1.0` scaling.
- **Paper-based training-free accelerations**:
  - **FlashAttention-2** (Dao, ICLR 2024, arXiv:2307.08691 §3.2–3.3): multi-warp Q-split & cooperative K/V shared-memory tiling (`half.cu`, `CudaSession.selectFusedAttention`).
  - **DeepCache + TaylorSeer** (Ma et al., CVPR 2024; Liu et al., ICCV 2025, arXiv:2503.06923): Newton divided-difference feature forecasting (`order = 0, 1, 2`) integrated into `DeepCache.scala` / `DeepCachePlugin.scala` (`DeepCache order` infotext key, default `0`) and Fast Mode (default `1`).
  - **T-GATE** (Zhang et al., TMLR 2024/2025, arXiv:2404.02747): temporal cross-attention caching after `gate_step` (`TGate.scala`, `TGatePlugin.scala`, `FastModePlugin.scala`, `python/sdfx/{hooks.py,engine.py,dryrun.py}`).
  - **Align Your Steps (AYS)** (Sabour et al., CVPR 2024, arXiv:2404.14507): direct discrete paper schedule & log-interpolated schedule for arbitrary `steps >= 1` wired onto `DPM++ 2M SDE`, `DPM++ 2M SDE Heun`, and `DPM++ 3M SDE` (`DpmSchedule.paperAys`, `NativeDiffusionBackend.scala`) with zero fallback warning.
  - **GPU-native Token Downsampling (ToDo)** (Smith et al., arXiv:2402.13573): `h_downsample_tokens_exact` / `CudaSession.downsampleTokensExact` eliminates CPU round-trips during self-attention key/value downsampling.

### Visual Inspection Notes (PNGs viewed directly via `read_file`)

- **Lossless baselines** (`sd15_base_b1`, `sdxl_base_b1`, `sdxl_base_b1_cudnn`): sharp linework, consistent anatomy, no numerical anomalies.
- **DeepCache / TaylorSeer**: `order=0` and `order=1` remain crisp and faithful on both SD1.5 and SDXL; `order=2` at `interval=3` overshoots highlights/saturation on SDXL and diverges structurally on SD1.5 (`DeepCachePlugin` defaults to `order=0` and `FastModePlugin` defaults to `order=1`).
- **T-GATE (`gate_step=8`)**: virtually indistinguishable from baseline on both SD1.5 and SDXL.
- **Fast Mode (`DC order=1 + T-GATE 8 + CFG 0.7`)**: sharp and coherent on SDXL (`4.784s`); coherent with a slight soft lighting halo on SD1.5 (`1.130s`).
- **GPU ToDo & AYS 10-step**: clean, crisp outputs on both SD1.5 (`0.932s` for AYS-10) and SDXL (`4.868s` for AYS-10) with zero engine fallback warnings. Note: on RTX 3090 fp16 with fused self-attention, ToDo `factor=2.0` adds asymmetric attention/downsampling dispatch overhead relative to packed-QKV baseline (`1.719s` vs `1.629s` on SD1.5; `8.703s` vs `8.345s` on SDXL) while still eliminating CPU round-trips.
