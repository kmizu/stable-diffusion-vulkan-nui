# Vulkan protocol and extension coverage

The Vulkan worker accepts JSON Lines protocol v1 requests. Its `hello.features` describes the running worker. The inventories below compare its handlers with `python/sdfx/server.py::QUEUED_TYPES` and `python/sdfx/hooks.py::KNOWN_HOOKS`. Some plugins run entirely in the desktop application.

### Fooocus Inpaint

The worker reads the original Fooocus v2.6 patch and head, broadcasts the per-channel or spatial quantization ranges, and merges matching deltas into a job-scoped SDXL U-Net. During the configured timestep window Vulkan convolves the mask and source VAE posterior mean with the head, then adds the result after the first U-Net convolution. The 768 × 768 SDXL check preserved every pixel outside the mask on Radeon 780M and RTX 3090. The worker removes the temporary U-Net after generation. [Model setup](WINDOWS_VULKAN.md) covers the required files and temporary space.

The staged Windows MCP executable also verified the four-channel SDXL checkpoint. A nine-channel SDXL Inpaint checkpoint ran on Radeon 780M and preserved unmasked pixels, with strong color artifacts inside the mask. [Verification](vulkan-worker-verification.md#windows-v046--fooocus-inpaint) records the fixtures and comparison.

### CD Tuner

The Vulkan graph scopes Detail and Contrast 1 to the selected steps and adjusts Contrast 2, Brightness and the three color axes at the configured denoised step. Color Map edits the regional latent input before prediction and compensates the denoised output. Saturation scales the original seven VAE decoder weight paths without writing to the checkpoint. Same-seed SD1.5 and SDXL runs confirmed distinct results for detail, color shift, Color Map and saturation on Radeon 780M and RTX 3090 respectively. Each edit preserved every source pixel outside the mask. Replaying after the active runs reproduced the stopped-detail control exactly.

RTX 3090 ran SDXL Latent Hires at 832 × 832 with separate Hires detail and stop settings. Detail also ran with a nine-channel SDXL Inpaint checkpoint at 768 × 768 and preserved the unmasked pixels. On Radeon 780M, changing Apply once affected the SD1.5 img2img Hires Fix and ADetailer follow-up passes.

Color Map evaluates the positive and negative branches separately. The worker computes each branch's own magnitude after previous region edits. The legacy backend measures both branches together. SD2.x and arbitrary combinations with other sampling hooks still need end-to-end coverage.

### T-GATE

The U-Net saves each cross-attention output at the gate step and reuses it on later steps, skipping its normalization and projections. The Vulkan buffer stays in the sampler session. Compatible batched CFG stores the mean of its two outputs. Separate predictions and intermediate solver evaluations have their own entries. Each Hires or img2img refinement pass starts with an empty cache. The cross-attention gate follows the [T-GATE method](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.02747).

SD1.5 on Radeon 780M saved and reused 16 outputs. SDXL on RTX 3090 saved and reused 70. The same-seed masked runs changed the edited region and retained every pixel outside it. A subsequent replay stayed exact after switching the seed and prompt. RTX 3090 also ran CFG 1, a combined scheduled CFG and Latent Hires pass, and Heun with distinct entries for the intermediate evaluations. Radeon 780M ran separate img2img Hires Fix.

### DeepCache

[DeepCache](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Ma_DeepCache_Accelerating_Diffusion_Models_for_Free_CVPR_2024_paper.html) skips the U-Net's deep downsampling, middle block and matching decoder blocks on reuse steps. The sampler owns the cached feature and the optional first and second divided differences for [TaylorSeer](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Liu_From_Reusing_to_Forecasting_Accelerating_Diffusion_Models_with_TaylorSeers_ICCV_2025_paper.html). The worker accepts cache interval, branch, initial full steps, forecast order and contraction. Relative L1 drift refresh is unavailable, and legacy DeepCache excludes ControlNet.

Radeon 780M ran masked SD1.5 at 512 × 512 and RTX 3090 ran masked SDXL at 768 × 768. Both logs confirmed feature reuse and both forecast orders. Same-seed tests retained every pixel outside the mask. SDXL repeated the cached run pixel for pixel and completed an 832 × 832 latent Hires pass. Constant reuse retained the appearance of the fox in the reviewed outputs. Forecasting produced pronounced color artifacts in the short SDXL run.

### Fast Mode

Fast Mode combines DeepCache, T-GATE, fixed-interval CFG-Cache and scheduled CFG in one sampler. It refreshes both CFG branches at scheduled DeepCache refreshes and at the T-GATE capture step. On conditional-only steps the positive stream can read its own slice of the paired DeepCache feature. The combined masked tests ran on SD1.5 at 512 × 512 on Radeon 780M and SDXL at 768 × 768 on RTX 3090. Both reproduced the same-seed image on replay and kept all unmasked pixels unchanged. RTX 3090 also completed an 832 × 832 latent Hires pass with all four hooks. Adaptive CFG-Cache and high-frequency boosting remain unavailable.

### Reference only

The worker crops the reference to the pass dimensions, encodes the VAE posterior mean and keeps a separate fixed noise tensor. Each evaluation captures the noised reference's normalized self-attention inputs on the Vulkan device. The target pass attends to both its own tokens and the weighted reference tokens. Style fidelity blends plain and reference attention on the positive branch. Reference only uses the existing checkpoint and VAE. It needs no adapter weights. The [original Reference-only description](https://github.com/Mikubill/sd-webui-controlnet/discussions/1236) explains the conditioning mechanism.

SDXL masked img2img on RTX 3090 exercised 70 self-attention layers per CFG branch and SD1.5 on Radeon 780M exercised 16. Reference selection and style fidelity changed the edited area in the SDXL test, and each masked output preserved the unmasked pixels. RTX 3090 also ran the nine-channel SDXL Inpaint checkpoint and a standard SDXL latent Hires pass. Reference-only captures add a U-Net pass per active CFG branch, and reference-active branches currently run separately. The v0.51 runtime also verified Canny with Reference only on Radeon 780M after isolating the reference hook from the ControlNet forward pass.

### Agent Attention

[Agent Attention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.08874) ranks source patches by cosine similarity to a destination token in each full stride window. The Vulkan inference graph merges selected sources by averaging, then runs ordinary attention on the reduced set or performs the two attention operations through the smaller agent set. It restores the full spatial token order before the rest of the U-Net block. Both stages accept independent strides and ratios. The sampler switches stages on consecutive distinct evaluation timesteps and restarts its seeded destination selection at the switch. The implementation uses the native random sequence and native tie ordering for equal cosine scores. Same-seed replays matched within this worker.

The masked SDXL test on RTX 3090 exercised ten layers with agents and ten with merge-only attention. It covered the FP32 path, switching or stopping mid-pass, exact same-seed replay and Hires-only application at 832 × 832. All unmasked pixels matched on the base-resolution cases. Radeon 780M exercised five layers with agents at 512 × 512 on SD1.5. Agent Attention with Canny completed masked SD1.5 and ordinary Hires on RTX 3090. The ordinary Hires graph also preserved the nine-channel SDXL Inpaint conditioning. Ineffective configurations keep ordinary generation pixel-exact, and the separate Img2Img Hires Fix stays outside Hires-only. Cross-attention remains unchanged.

### ControlNet-LLLite

The native worker reads the SDXL attention-projection adapters from the selected model file. Each adapter encodes the RGB hint, combines it with the projection input and adds the learned residual before Q, K or V. Conditioning stays in the Vulkan runner for the current sampling pass. The worker honors the strength and timestep interval and uses the prepared img2img input when the hint is omitted. A zero strength or reversed interval uses the normal generation path. Reference-only can use the same hint for each of its two spatial token grids. [Official model repository](https://huggingface.co/kohya-ss/controlnet-lllite).

The official Canny model applied 136 projections per evaluation in the standalone SDXL Vulkan tests. RTX 3090 exercised reference attention, a second published adapter, a partial timestep window and the nine-channel Inpaint checkpoint at 832 × 832 in Hires. Radeon 780M ran SDXL masked img2img at 512 × 512. The masked comparisons preserved all source pixels outside the edit, and a later same-seed Canny replay matched pixel for pixel. LLLite also ran with ordinary Canny in the separate Img2Img Hires Fix.

### LLuL

LLuL crops a selected U-Net feature region, enlarges it for a second layer evaluation and blends the reduced result back into the original region. The Vulkan graph supports the output layer, residual blocks, spatial transformers, self-attention and cross-attention. It uses the configured evaluation window, layer filters, region and scale factor. The resize operators support nearest-exact, bilinear, bicubic, area and fixed-window pooling, with antialiasing for bilinear and bicubic. The optional grayscale blend mask is independent of the img2img paint mask. The spherical blend evaluates an angle approximation in FP32. [Original LLuL extension](https://github.com/hnmr293/sd-webui-llul).

The packaged executable ran masked SD1.5 and SDXL img2img through MCP on Radeon 780M and RTX 3090 respectively. Direct tests also covered the four advanced layer types, odd-width feature grids and both SDXL Latent Hires passes. [Verification and tested limits](vulkan-worker-verification.md#windows-v053--llul).

Further Python generation hooks without a corresponding verified implementation in this inventory include `aesthetic_gradients`, `characteristic_guidance`, `compile`, `composable_lora` and `incantations`.

### Boundaries of the port

The pinned Vulkan library exposes complete diffusion graphs, vision, VAE, upscaler and motion components. The packaged build extends its vision runner to CLIP ViT-L and ViT-bigG. The U-Net patches expose FreeU, PAG, HyperTile and the resizing hooks above. The SAG hook follows the legacy SD1.x txt2img scope and is skipped for img2img. WD14 captioning uses a separately installed tagger. GFPGAN and character search use separately installed models. Novel-view synthesis requires its own inference model. The entries above identify which operations currently have a worker implementation.

The per-step latent graphs currently support Euler and Euler a. The two prediction-level CFG hooks require Euler. Custom graphs reject unsupported combinations of ControlNet, embeddings, high-resolution refinement and other sampling hooks. Low-resolution five-step tests establish that the model, sampler and VAE paths run. Some strongly graded or averaged results were blurry. The 768 x 768 IP-Adapter comparison used twelve steps and was inspected alongside a same-seed baseline. Fifteen-step 768 x 768 SDXL comparisons also produced recognizable fox images with default Detail Daemon and PostDiff, including the expected composition change from the lower-resolution first stage.
